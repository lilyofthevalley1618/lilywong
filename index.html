Featured project

Zapling

Zapling is a gamified electronics-learning app I built. It turns circuits into bite-sized lessons with streaks, XP and flashcards, plus a hands-on Circuit Lab and Sprig, a built-in helper that answers questions as you go.

  • Learning path from charge basics to real circuits
  • Circuit Lab for building and testing loops
  • Streaks, XP and spaced-review flashcards
